# Demo script (record a 60-90s Loom, send to prospects)

Keep it under 90 seconds. Show, don't tell. Record with a spare WhatsApp number as the "bot".

## Shot list

**[0:00-0:10] Hook (face or screen)**
> "Hi, I'm Kunal. I built a WhatsApp assistant that answers your customers instantly — even
> at 11 PM when you're closed — and saves every lead to a sheet."

**[0:10-0:25] Screen: message the bot**
- From a second phone, send: *"Hi, aapka rate kya hai?"*
- Point at the screen: the bot replies in ~2 seconds.

**[0:25-0:50] Screen: watch it qualify**
- The bot asks 2-3 questions (service, budget, timeline).
- Type answers. Show it books / confirms.

**[0:50-1:05] Screen: the lead appears**
- Switch to the Google Sheet (or dashboard). The new lead is there with all answers.
- "Every enquiry is captured. Nothing gets lost, nothing waits."

**[1:05-1:20] Close**
> "I set this up on YOUR WhatsApp number in under a day. If you want it, message me —
> I'll show it live on a call. Price depends on what you need."

## Rules while recording
- Use a **spare number**, default config, so nothing private shows.
- Blur any real customer names/numbers before sending.
- Do **not** claim specific results ("this gets 50 sales"). Say "answers every customer instantly".
- One take is fine. Loom free tier is enough.

## Send template (WhatsApp)
> Hi [Name], I make a WhatsApp assistant for [their business type] — it replies to customers
> instantly and saves every lead. Here's a 90-sec demo: [Loom link]
> If useful, I can set it up on your number in a day. If not, no problem — just say "No".
